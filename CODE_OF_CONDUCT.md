# Code of Conduct

This is a learning community. People arrive with very different backgrounds and levels of experience, and everyone should feel safe asking "basic" questions.

## Our standards
**Do**
- Be welcoming, patient and kind, especially to beginners.
- Give constructive, specific feedback (the same way the project teaches code reviews).
- Assume good intent, and accept correction gracefully.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

**Don't**
- Harass, insult or demean anyone, for any reason: experience level, background, identity, language or anything else.
- Post other people's private information.
- Spam, self-promote aggressively, or post full solutions in public threads (it spoils the learning for others; use spoiler tags or keep them in your own repo).
- Engage in conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope
This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any community channels linked from the README.

## Enforcement
Report problems to the maintainers by opening a confidential
[security advisory](https://github.com/vin2k20/learn-mern-by-building/security/advisories/new) (it's private),
or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and warn or ban participants,
as needed to keep the community welcoming. Reports are handled confidentially.
